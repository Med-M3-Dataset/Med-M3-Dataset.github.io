Med-M3 Dataset

Med-M3 Dataset: A Medical Video Dataset with Multi-lingual, Multi-model and Multi-hop

中文 | English

Overview

This dataset is a large-scale video dataset in Chinese and English, and its multimodal feature is not only reflected in the video input information, but also includes some of the questions that we have designed to be solved by the model that require cross-modal knowledge graph construction and multi-hop thinking ability, which are called "complex questions", and correspond to "simple questions".

The annotation data is stored in MedM3.json.

Dataset Formats

  
                  
{  
    "id": 1,  
    "video_id": "Gm9UTA9jmdU",  
    "question": "如何通过脚趾和跟腱的拉伸来纠正扁平足？",  
    "start": 451,  
    "end": 484,  
    "complex": 0,  
    "language": "Chinese"  
}
  • The "id" field is the ID of the question.
  • The "video_id" field is the video ID on YouTube, due to YouTube policy and other reasons, we cannot provide the video itself directly, please download it yourself through PyTube or other ways to get.
  • The "question" field is the specific content of the question.
  • The "start" and "end" fields are the timestamps of the two videos, which refer to the number of start seconds and the end seconds of the video clip in the video that can be used to answer the above questions.
  • The "complex" field indicates whether the problem is complex or not. A field of 0 indicates that the problem is simple, and a field of 1 indicates that the problem is complex.
  • "language" refers to the language of the question.

Tasks

We designed two tasks for this dataset:

  1. Retrieval: Given a question and a corresponding video, the challenger needs to give a time-stamped video clip to ensure that the clip answers the corresponding question, as shown in the image:

  2. Choosing and Retrieval: Given a question and multiple videos, the challenger needs to find a video with a topic that matches the description of the question, and then give a time-stamped video clip to make sure that the clip answers the corresponding question, as shown in the image:

Other Files

For ease of use, we provide additional subtitle files and the knowledge graph schema we have built for reference, of course, you can also generate your own information to input to the model when using the dataset.

  • Subtitles are stored in Subtitles.json (compressed in volumes because the file size exceeds Github's size limit).
  • The knowledge graph is stored in KnowledgeGraph.json .
